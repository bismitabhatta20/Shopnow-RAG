# Consumer Rights for Customers in the United Kingdom

## 14-day cancellation right
Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you can cancel an online purchase **within 14 days of delivery** for any reason, and you then have a further 14 days to send the goods back. We refund the price and standard delivery within 14 days.

## Faulty goods (Consumer Rights Act 2015)
- **Within 30 days** of delivery: short-term right to reject faulty goods for a full refund.
- **Within 6 months**: right to a repair or replacement; if that fails, a refund (it is presumed the fault existed at delivery).
- **Up to 6 years** (5 in Scotland): you may still claim for faults, but you may need to show the fault was present at delivery.

## Exceptions
Perishable goods, sealed hygiene goods once unsealed, and personalised items are excluded from the cancellation right (but not from faulty-goods rights).
