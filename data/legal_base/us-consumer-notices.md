# Consumer Notices for Customers in the United States

## Warranties
Our limited warranty is provided under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. Implied warranties of merchantability are limited to the duration of the written warranty (1 year) where state law allows; some states do not allow such limits, so they may not apply to you.

## Chargebacks
You may dispute a charge with your card issuer under the Fair Credit Billing Act. We encourage you to contact us first — most issues are resolved faster than a chargeback. While a chargeback is open, the related order cannot be refunded by us a second time.

## Return window
There is no general federal law granting a right to return goods purchased online; returns are governed by our Returns and Refunds Policy (30 days for most items).

## California
California residents may request disclosure of personal information shared with third parties for direct marketing. See the Privacy Policy for CCPA/CPRA rights.
