# Consumer Rights for Customers in the European Union

## Right of withdrawal (14 days)
Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive you may withdraw from a purchase **within 14 days of delivery without giving any reason**. This applies even to products whose standard store return window is shorter (for example the 10-day window for wearables).
- We refund all payments received, **including the standard delivery cost**, within 14 days of being informed of your withdrawal. We may withhold the refund until we receive the goods back or you provide proof of return.
- The right of withdrawal does not apply to: sealed goods unsealed for health or hygiene reasons, perishable goods, personalised goods, and digital content once download has started with your consent.

## Legal guarantee (2 years)
Sellers are liable for any lack of conformity that becomes apparent **within 2 years of delivery**. You may ask for repair or replacement free of charge, and if that is impossible or fails, a price reduction or refund. This is in addition to our commercial warranty.

## Online dispute resolution
You may use the alternative dispute resolution body in your member state.
