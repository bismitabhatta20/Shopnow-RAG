# Privacy Policy and Data Rights

## Data we collect
Account and contact details, addresses, order history, support conversations, device and usage data. Payment card data is processed by our PCI-certified payment processor; we store only a token, the card brand, last four digits and expiry.

## How we use data
To fulfil orders, provide support, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations (for example tax records), and — with your consent — send marketing.

## Retention
Order and invoice records are kept for **7 years** for tax and accounting obligations, even after account deletion. Support conversations are kept for 2 years.

## Your rights
- **Access / export**: request a copy of your personal data (delivered within 30 days).
- **Correction** of inaccurate data.
- **Deletion** ("right to be forgotten"): we delete or anonymise your account within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
- **Opt out of marketing** at any time via the unsubscribe link or account settings.
- **EU/UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)**: you may also object to processing, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
- **California (CCPA/CPRA)**: right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

## Making a request
Privacy requests are handled by our privacy team, not in chat, because we must verify your identity. Send your request to privacy@shop.example.com or use Account → Privacy → Request my data; the privacy team responds within 30 days (45 days for CCPA where extended).
