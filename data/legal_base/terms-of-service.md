# Terms of Service

## Contract formation
A contract is formed when we confirm your order by email (status "paid"). We may cancel orders affected by obvious pricing errors before shipment and refund you in full.

## Pricing errors
If an item is listed at an obviously incorrect price, we are not obliged to sell at that price, but we will notify you and offer to complete the order at the correct price or cancel it.

## Limitation of liability
To the extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim relating to a product is limited to the amount you paid for that product. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, or rights that cannot be excluded by law (including statutory consumer rights in the EU and UK).

## Disputes (United States)
US customers agree to resolve disputes through **individual binding arbitration** administered by the AAA, except small-claims court matters. You may **opt out of arbitration within 30 days** of first accepting these terms by writing to legal@shop.example.com. Class actions are waived to the extent permitted by law.

## Disputes (EU and UK)
EU and UK customers may bring claims in the courts of their country of residence. EU customers may also use alternative dispute resolution; we participate in the consumer ADR scheme in the customer's member state.

## Account suspension
We may suspend accounts involved in fraud, abuse of the returns policy (for example, excessive returns of used goods), or chargeback abuse. You can appeal a suspension by contacting support.

## Governing law
US: laws of the State of Delaware. EU/UK: the mandatory consumer protection laws of your country of residence apply.

## Changes
We notify you by email at least 30 days before material changes take effect.
