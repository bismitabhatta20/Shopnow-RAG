# Pulse Smartwatch (SKU-SW-300)

## Battery life
Up to 7 days typical use, 30 hours with continuous GPS. Battery drains faster with always-on display enabled.

## Water resistance
Rated 5 ATM: suitable for swimming in pools and shallow water, not for diving or hot showers.

## Returns
Wearables have a **10-day return window** from delivery (EU/UK customers: 14 days statutory). Defects are covered by the 1-year warranty.

## Sync problems
Restart the watch and the phone, make sure the Pulse app has background refresh enabled, then re-sync.
