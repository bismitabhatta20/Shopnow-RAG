# Aurora Wireless Headphones (SKU-HP-100)

## Pairing
Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the LED flashes blue and white, then choose "Aurora" in your device's Bluetooth settings. Up to 2 devices can be connected at once.

## Reset
If pairing fails, hold power + volume down for 10 seconds until the LED flashes red three times, then pair again.

## Battery
Up to 40 hours with ANC off, 30 hours with ANC on. A 10-minute USB-C charge gives 5 hours of playback.

## Common issues
- One side silent: reset the headphones and re-pair.
- Crackling with ANC: update firmware via the Aurora app.
If the issue persists after a reset, it is likely a defect — start a return with reason "defective" (covered by the 1-year warranty).
