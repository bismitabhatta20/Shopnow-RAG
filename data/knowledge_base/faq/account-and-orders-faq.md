# Account and Orders FAQ

## How do I track my order?
Your tracking number is in the order details (Account → Orders) and in the shipping confirmation email. Click the tracking number to see live carrier updates.

## How do I place, cancel or change an order?
Orders are placed at checkout. To cancel an order or change its shipping address, open Account → Orders, select the order and choose "Cancel order" or "Change address" (available until the order ships). The support assistant answers questions about our policies but cannot view or change your orders.

## How do I start a return?
Open Account → Orders, select the delivered order and choose "Return items". You will receive a prepaid label by email.

## Why was my order cancelled?
Orders may be cancelled if payment authorisation fails, an item is out of stock after ordering, or our fraud checks flag the order. You are never charged for an order we cancel.

## How do I change my email or password?
Go to Account → Security. 

## Do you price match?
We don't match competitors' prices, but we offer price adjustments if our own price drops within 7 days of purchase.

## How do I talk to a person?
Email support@shop.example.com or use the "Contact us" form in your account. Typical response: within 24 hours (urgent issues within 1 hour). Privacy requests go to privacy@shop.example.com; legal notices to legal@shop.example.com.
