# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Costs
- Standard shipping costs **$5.99**. Orders with a subtotal of **$50 or more ship free**.
- Express shipping is available at checkout for $14.99 in selected areas.

## Delivery times
- Orders are processed within 1 business day and handed to the carrier (UPS, FedEx, USPS, DHL or Royal Mail).
- Standard delivery: 3–5 business days (US), 4–7 business days (EU/UK).

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships and is visible in the order details.

## Late, lost or stolen packages
- If tracking shows no movement for **5 business days**, or the package is marked delivered but you cannot find it after 48 hours, contact support. We open a carrier investigation and, if the parcel is not found within 7 days, we send a replacement or a full refund.
- Damaged deliveries: keep the packaging and report within 7 days with photos.
