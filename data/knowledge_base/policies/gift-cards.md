# Gift Cards

- Gift cards never expire and have no fees.
- Gift cards can't be returned, refunded or exchanged for cash, except where required by law (for example, some US states require cash redemption of balances under $10 on request).
- Lost or stolen gift cards can be replaced only if you can provide the order number they were purchased with and the code has not yet been redeemed.
- If an order paid with a gift card is cancelled or returned, the refund goes back to the gift card balance.
