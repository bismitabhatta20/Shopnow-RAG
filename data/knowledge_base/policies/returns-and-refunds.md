# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for any reason, as long as they are unused, in original packaging and with all accessories.
Some products have a shorter window, shown on the product page and in the order details:
- Pulse Smartwatch and other wearables: **10 days** from delivery (hygiene and battery-cycle reasons).
Customers in the EU and UK always have at least the statutory **14-day right of withdrawal** from delivery, even where the store window is shorter (see the regional consumer-rights pages).

## Items that cannot be returned
- **Final-sale / clearance** items marked "Final sale".
- **Gift cards** (digital or physical) — they are neither returnable nor refundable for cash, except where required by law.
- **Perishable goods** such as coffee beans and food once delivered.
- Personalised or custom-made items.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged, is defective or is not what you ordered, you can return it **free of charge within 1 year of delivery**, even after the normal return window, under our warranty. Choose the reason "damaged", "defective" or "wrong item" when starting the return. We may ask for a photo.

## How to start a return
1. Open the order in your account (Account → Orders → Return items) and choose the items to return and a reason.
2. You receive a **prepaid return label** by email immediately after approval.
3. Ship the items within **14 days** of approval.

## Refunds
- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**. We cannot redirect refunds to a different card.
- We issue the refund within **5 business days after the returned items are received** at our warehouse. Your bank may take a further 3–10 business days to show it.
- The refund covers the item price plus the tax paid on it. Original shipping fees are refunded only for damaged, defective or wrong items (and for full order withdrawals in the EU/UK).
- Partial returns are refunded proportionally.

## Exchanges
We don't process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order; we can expedite the new order's shipping on request for size exchanges.
