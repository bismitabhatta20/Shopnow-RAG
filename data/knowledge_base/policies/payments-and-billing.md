# Payments and Billing

## Accepted payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and store gift cards.

## Card security
For your security, **our support team and assistant never ask for or accept full card numbers in chat, email or by phone**. New cards are added only through our secure, PCI-compliant payment page (Account → Payment methods → Add card).

## When you are charged
Your card is authorised when you place the order and charged when the order is confirmed (status "paid"). Pending orders are not charged.

## Updating billing details
You can update the billing address of a saved card, choose a different default card, or switch the card used on an order that is still pending or paid.

## Unrecognised or duplicate charges
A pending authorisation may appear twice briefly; duplicates drop off within 3–5 business days. If a duplicate charge has settled, contact support with the order number and we refund it within 2 business days.

## Price adjustments
If the price of an item drops within **7 days** of your purchase, we refund the difference once per item on request. Clearance and final-sale items are excluded.

## Invoices and taxes
VAT invoices are available for EU and UK orders from the order page. US sales tax is calculated by delivery address.
