# Product Warranty

- All electronics sold by us carry a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date covering manufacturing defects. Apparel and home goods are covered for 1 year against defects in materials and workmanship.
- Warranty claims within the first year: we replace or refund the item at our choice; return shipping is free (choose reason "defective" when starting a return).
- Not covered: accidental damage, water damage beyond the stated rating, normal wear, unauthorised repairs.
- EU and UK customers additionally keep their statutory guarantee rights (2 years in the EU; up to 6 years in the UK) — see the regional consumer-rights pages.
